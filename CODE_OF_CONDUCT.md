# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in these repositories to be a straightforward, harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Standards

Things that help:

- welcoming and inclusive language
- respect for differing viewpoints and experience levels
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the people using this code

Things that do not:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind
- trolling, insults, and personal or political attacks
- harassment, public or private
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in these repositories and anywhere someone is representing them in public.

Report unacceptable behaviour to **hello@quanticdata.io**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions that do not follow this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone for behaviour they consider inappropriate.
